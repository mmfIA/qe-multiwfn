=================

Highlights
- Backward-compatible 0.1.1 ABI: 17 original qe_* symbols unchanged
  (version node QE_MULTIWFN_0.1.1).
- Additive Molecular Data Model 1.0 ABI: 12 qe_v1_* symbols
  (version node QE_MULTIWFN_0.2.0).
- ORCA 6.1.1 JSON adapter for RHF/UHF/ROHF with s/p, spherical d/f and
  Cartesian d/f support (Cartesian validated with a PySCF-generated fixture).
- Canonical model with shells, primitives, exponents, contraction
  coefficients, AO map, provenance and strict validation.
- Opt-in UHF/ROHF execution; ROHF validated against Multiwfn and ORCA.
- P0 RHF results preserved: H2/H2O/C6H6 Mayer matrices bit-for-bit.
